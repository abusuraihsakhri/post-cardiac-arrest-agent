Temperature-control guidance applies to adults unresponsive to verbal commands after ROSC (32–37.5 °C; at least 36 hours is reasonable). AHA recommends MAP ≥65 mm Hg. Neurologic prognosis requires delayed, multimodal assessment, including consideration of confounding.
